Useful first.
Always kind.

01

Share what you know

02

Protect sensitive places

Do not expose restricted access, fragile habitats, cultural sites, or unsafe unofficial routes.

03

Respect the community

Keep discussion constructive, welcoming, relevant, and free of harassment or spam.
