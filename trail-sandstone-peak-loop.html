Sandstone Peak Loop via Mishe Mokwa

A high-value Santa Monica Mountains loop linking Split Rock, Balanced Rock, the Backbone Trail, and the range's 3,111-foot high point.

6.0 milesDistance1,075 feetElevation gainModerate to strenuousDifficulty

The Mishe Mokwa route earns its mileage with constant variety: chaparral, shaded rock, improbable formations, and broad Pacific-facing ridges. The final summit spur is short but rocky, and the reward is one of the widest views in the range.

ROUTE WALKTHROUGH

What the hike is like

  1. Start from the Sandstone Peak Trailhead and use the connector to reach the Mishe Mokwa Trail.
  2. Follow Mishe Mokwa past Carlisle Canyon, Balanced Rock, and the shaded Split Rock area before joining the Backbone Trail.
  3. Turn toward Sandstone Peak, take the signed summit spur, then descend on the Sandstone Peak Trail to close the loop.

BEFORE YOU DRIVE

Planning the day

  • The NPS Circle X map lists this loop at 6 miles and the direct summit trail at 3 miles; choose the full loop for the more varied day.
  • There is little reliable shade after Split Rock and no dependable drinking water. Start early on warm days.
  • Parking is limited along winding Yerba Buena Road. Do not block gates or road shoulders.

TURN-AROUND JUDGMENT

Safety that matters here

  • Expect loose rock and a short, hands-on summit section.
  • Heat, wind, wildfire restrictions, and wet-weather closures can change the plan quickly.
  • Download the official map because several trail junctions branch toward longer Backbone Trail segments.
