# Trailbound SoCal Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Trailbound SoCal is committed to maintaining a welcoming, safe, and constructive community. Everyone participating in the project deserves to be treated with dignity and respect, regardless of identity, background, experience, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Project participants should:

- Communicate with patience, honesty, and respect.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of technical or outdoor experience.
- Give specific, constructive feedback without attacking the person providing the contribution.
- Accept reasonable corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Credit sources for submitted trail, campsite, map, or safety information.
- Protect personal information and respect clearly stated boundaries.
- Prioritize accurate, responsible outdoor information.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following conduct is not permitted:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or unwanted personal attention.
- Discriminatory, degrading, insulting, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, deliberate disruption, trolling, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing another person’s private information without permission.
- Impersonation, deliberate misinformation, or failure to credit copied material.
- Encouraging unsafe, illegal, or environmentally destructive outdoor behavior.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone is publicly representing Trailbound SoCal.

## Reporting a concern

Do not place sensitive conduct reports in a public GitHub issue. Contact the project maintainer through a private contact method listed on the [cdevine-dev GitHub profile](https://github.com/cdevine-dev).

Include only the information needed to understand the incident. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily limit participation, or permanently block a participant when necessary to protect the project and its community. The response will reflect the seriousness, context, and pattern of the behavior.

People involved in reviewing a report should avoid conflicts of interest and protect the privacy of everyone involved.
