REGIONAL FIELD NOTE

Know the landscape

San Diego hiking changes character quickly as you move inland. Coastal routes offer breezes and sandstone, while foothill climbs rise through boulder fields and sun-exposed chaparral toward wide county views.

Heat is the defining constraint on inland routes. Local agencies may close trails during excessive-heat warnings, and water is rarely available beyond developed parks.

Plan for this region

  • Choose coast or elevation by the forecast
  • Expect busy weekend trailheads
  • Treat heat closures as hard stops
