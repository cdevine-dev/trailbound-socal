Borrego Palm Canyon Nature Trail

A rocky wash climbs toward a native California fan-palm oasis beneath the walls of one of the state's largest desert parks.

3.0 milesDistanceAbout 700 feetElevation gainModerateDifficulty
CURRENT CONDITION NOTE

Current State Parks guidance says the palm grove is closed until further notice. The 1.5-mile approach remains open to an overlook where visitors can see post-fire palm recovery. Verify this notice on the official page before hiking.

This nature trail is a compact introduction to Anza-Borrego geology, desert plants, flash-flood terrain, and Peninsular bighorn habitat. The destination has changed after fire, so the official condition notice matters more than older trip reports.

ROUTE WALKTHROUGH

What the hike is like

  1. Start at Borrego Palm Canyon Campground and follow the numbered nature-trail route up the rocky wash.
  2. The trail climbs gradually through boulders, desert willow, and fan-palm habitat toward the canyon's upper section.
  3. Observe all closure signs. The current public route reaches a recovery overlook before returning the same way.

BEFORE YOU DRIVE

Planning the day

  • California State Parks lists the full nature trail at 3 miles round trip with roughly 700 feet of gain and a minimum two-hour time estimate.
  • Stop at the visitor center for current maps, water planning, and closure details.
  • Choose a cool day. Even spring afternoons can become dangerously hot in the canyon.

TURN-AROUND JUDGMENT

Safety that matters here

  • Never enter the wash when thunderstorms or flash floods are possible, including storms far upstream.
  • Carry and drink plenty of water; shade is limited for most of the approach.
  • Watch wildlife from a distance and never approach bighorn sheep or enter closed habitat.
