REGIONAL FIELD NOTE

Know the landscape

Orange County's coastal parks protect a surprising network of canyons and ridges within sight of the Pacific. Routes often use wide fire roads, narrow connectors, and shared-use trails that can be combined into many loop lengths.

The marine layer can soften a summer morning, but exposed ridges heat quickly. Day-use fees, sunset closing times, and post-rain trail closures are common.

Plan for this region

  • Use the official park map to choose a loop
  • Respect rain closures
  • Plan time for the beach after the hike
