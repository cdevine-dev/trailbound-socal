Potato Chip Rock via Lake Poway

A steep, sun-exposed climb from Lake Poway to Mount Woodson's famous granite ledge and broad inland San Diego views.

WATER SOURCE

Carry all water

Developed water may be available near Lake Poway facilities, but there is no dependable water on the climb. Fill before leaving the recreation area and carry the entire hike's supply.

The photo is famous, but the climb is the real story. From Lake Poway the route gains roughly 2,000 feet through chaparral and granite boulder fields, with very little shade and a final summit-area walk to the rock.

ROUTE WALKTHROUGH

What the hike is like

  1. Begin on the Lake Poway Loop and follow signs for Mount Woodson Trail at the east side of the lake.
  2. Climb steadily through switchbacks and boulder country toward the Mount Woodson summit area.
  3. Continue to Potato Chip Rock, wait safely for your turn if there is a line, then return by the same route.

BEFORE YOU DRIVE

Planning the day

  • The City of Poway guide lists the Mount Woodson Trail itself as 2.4 miles with about 2,000 feet of gain; the Lake Poway approach adds distance.
  • Weekend parking fills early. Check the city's current Lake Poway hours and fee schedule.
  • Bring all the water you need. The route is exposed almost from start to finish.

TURN-AROUND JUDGMENT

Safety that matters here

  • Poway may close exposed trails during excessive heat. Never route around a heat closure.
  • The rock photo requires a scramble and has a drop below it. Skip the pose if it is crowded, windy, wet, or outside your comfort zone.
  • Rattlesnakes and loose granite are normal backcountry hazards; watch your footing and hand placement.
