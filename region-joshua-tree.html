REGIONAL FIELD NOTE

Know the landscape

Joshua Tree's trail system crosses two deserts and a huge elevation range. The most rewarding days pair a focused hike with time to read the landscape: Pinto gneiss, monzogranite, Joshua tree woodland, washes, and distant mountain silhouettes.

Fall through spring is the main hiking season. Parking can fill during holidays, pets are not allowed on hiking trails, and summer heat makes even short objectives hazardous.

Plan for this region

  • Carry all water from the trailhead
  • Use designated parking only
  • Check park alerts and entrance conditions
