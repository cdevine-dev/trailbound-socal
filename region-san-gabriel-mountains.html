REGIONAL FIELD NOTE

Know the landscape

The San Gabriels rise abruptly from the foothill cities, creating everything from boulder-filled river canyons to alpine summit routes. Short map distances can hide thousands of feet of climbing and rapid changes in temperature.

The range is also shaped by wildfire, flood, and active restoration. Forest orders, damaged roads, winter ice, and flash-flood risk are core planning factors rather than last-minute details.

Plan for this region

  • Read Angeles National Forest alerts before every trip
  • Carry offline navigation
  • Match winter objectives to real mountaineering skills
